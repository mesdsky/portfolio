Energy innovation · 2025

Eco-Power from Waste

As a member of team artWorker, I helped develop a simulated dual-chamber Microbial Fuel Cell concept using food waste as a carbon source and drilling mud as the bioremediation target.

ProblemTwo significant waste streams remain underutilized and environmentally harmful.
ApproachModel biological kinetics and electrical output as one integrated MFC system.
SystemMFC stack, DC–DC boost converter, sodium-ion battery, and monitoring sensors.
