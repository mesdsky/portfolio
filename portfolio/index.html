2025 / ENERGY INNOVATION

Eco-Power from Waste

As a member of team artWorker, I helped develop a simulated dual-chamber Microbial Fuel Cell system using food waste as a carbon source and drilling mud as the bioremediation target. The team’s proposal earned the Favorite Idea title at the 2025 Innovation Idea Competition by Pertamina Hulu Energi.

The ideaProduce low-power renewable electricity while treating two waste streams at once.
The systemMFC stack → DC–DC boost converter → sodium-ion battery → mining-monitoring sensors.
My roleCollaborative research, system development, simulation analysis, and competition delivery as a team member.
Next stepValidate the MATLAB/Simulink model through a laboratory-scale experiment.
