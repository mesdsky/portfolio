# Design Parameters — V3

- Direction: premium dark portfolio inspired by the supplied reference, adapted for an electrical-engineering profile.
- Palette: carbon `#11110F`, surface `#1B1B18`, orange `#FF5A1F`, warm cream `#E8DFCC`, white `#F5F1E8`.
- Typography: bold modern sans for structure; restrained Georgia italic for expressive emphasis.
- Layout: rounded shell navigation, split hero, three-card expertise grid, large case-study panel, structured experience timeline, cream biography card.
- Content hierarchy: identity → capabilities → proof of work → leadership → personal context → contact.
- Motion: minimal transform feedback only; reduced-motion supported.
- Avoid: fake projects, invented metrics, generic testimonials, glowing gradients, glass cards, and decorative 3D imagery.
