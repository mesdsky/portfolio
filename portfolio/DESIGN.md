# Design Parameters

- **Concept:** Signal / Noise — clear ideas presented with editorial restraint.
- **Palette:** Paper `#F3F1EA`, Ink `#11110F`, Muted `#6B6962`, Cobalt `#2447FF`, Rule `#CBC8BE`.
- **Type:** Arial/Helvetica for display and body; ui-monospace for labels and metadata.
- **Grid:** max 1180px; 12 columns desktop; 24px gutters; single column under 760px.
- **Shape:** square and lightly rounded surfaces only; no pill-heavy interface.
- **Motion:** 180–600ms, transform/opacity only; disabled with `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Imagery:** code-native abstract diagrams per project until genuine screenshots or photography are supplied.
- **Voice:** short, direct, specific. No buzzword soup.
